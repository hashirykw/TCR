Sitting in 2027? Three things changed.

  1. Business Studies 7115 is gone. O Level students now sit Business 7081.
  2. New Economics 2281 and Accounting 7707, with longer multiple-choice papers.
  3. A Level Urdu moves to 9866, the Pakistan-only Language & Literature syllabus.

From O Level to Matric, A Level to Inter.

Matric equivalence needs eight O Level subjects at grade E or above, with Maths, English, Urdu, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies compulsory. Inter needs three relevant A Levels: Physics, Chemistry and Maths for Pre-Engineering; add Biology for Pre-Medical.

Ask on WhatsApp
Which exam board do you teach?

Cambridge International (CAIE): O Level, AS Level and A2 Level. Every course follows the syllabus code that applies to the 2027 exams, listed in the syllabus library above.

When are classes held?

Monday to Saturday, in the afternoon and evening after school. Most sciences run as Friday–Saturday blocks; commerce subjects run on Tuesday–Thursday. Sunday is closed on campus.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. AS and A2 Psychology and Sociology are taught online. Every other subject is taught on campus.

Can you help with university entry tests?

Yes. We prepare students for the IBA, IOBM, LUMS, NUST, GIKI and FAST admission tests, alongside application help, personal statements and interview practice.

What is the student portal?

Enrolled students sign in at the-classroom.learn360lms.com for recorded lectures, online tests and class material. Your login is issued when you enrol.

How do I reserve a seat?

Use Reserve a seat on this page. Pick your level and subjects, and your request opens in WhatsApp with the front desk, who confirm your room and timing.
