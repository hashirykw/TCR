Every paper, every topic.

The official Cambridge syllabus for each subject we teach, in the version that applies to the 2027 exams. Tap a code to open its papers and topics.

Sitting in 2027? Three things changed.

  1. Business Studies 7115 is gone. O Level students now sit Business 7081.
  2. New Economics 2281 and Accounting 7707, with longer multiple-choice papers.
  3. A Level Urdu moves to 9866, the Pakistan-only Language & Literature syllabus.

What an A* actually took.

There is no single “90% for an A*”. Cambridge’s official overall thresholds from June 2026 swing from 68% in Islamiyat to 92% in A Level Maths. Ranges cover every paper variant.

From O Level to Matric, A Level to Inter.

Matric equivalence needs eight O Level subjects at grade E or above, with Maths, English, Urdu, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies compulsory. Inter needs three relevant A Levels: Physics, Chemistry and Maths for Pre-Engineering; add Biology for Pre-Medical.
